# ESI-Style 5-Level Triage Rubric (Domain Primer)

Source-style: Emergency Severity Index (ESI) v4, adapted for **intake-form-only OPD triage**
(no labs, imaging, or ECG at triage). Level **1 = most urgent**.

## Level 1 — Resuscitation (see NOW, resurrect room)
- Requires immediate life-saving intervention.
- Intake proxies: unresponsive / cardiac arrest / apnoeic / severe respiratory distress with SpO₂ <90 / profound shock (SBP <80, MAP <55) / HR <35 or >150 with instability / severe bleeding / anaphylaxis / status seizure / major trauma.
- Target: bedside <1 min.

## Level 2 — Emergent (high risk, <15 min)
- High-risk situation, confused/lethargic/disoriented, severe pain/distress, deranged vitals needing rapid workup but not yet resuscitation.
- Intake proxies: chest pain with red-flag accompaniments (sweating, breathlessness, radiation to arm/jaw, nausea in risk patient) / stroke signs (face droop, arm drift, slurred speech, sudden vision loss) / SpO₂ 90–92 with distress / SBP >220 or DBP >130 / pregnant + severe headache + HTN / labour with bleeding / infant <3 mo febrile ≥38 °C / suicidal with plan.
- Target: <15 min.

## Level 3 — Urgent (<60 min)
- Stable vitals but needs ≥2 resources / admission likely; moderate distress.
- Proxies: high fever (≥39 °C) with comorbidity / elderly + tachycardia + hypotension trend / persistent vomiting-diarrhoea with moderate dehydration / fracture with neurovascular intact / severe sore throat with drooling excluded.
- Target: <60 min.

## Level 4 — Less urgent (<120 min)
- Stable, needs 1 resource (suture, X-ray, prescription refill with mild abnormality).
- Proxies: mild fever / cold-cough without distress / minor cut / sprain / single-complaint stable vitals.
- Target: <120 min.

## Level 5 — Non-urgent (<240 min)
- Stable, needs no resource or routine: medication refill, chronic stable follow-up, mild self-limiting symptoms, normal vitals.
- Target: <240 min.

## Tie-break rule
When in doubt between two levels, **choose the more urgent level**. Under-triage kills; over-triage queues.
Absence of vitals must NOT reassure — missing data raises, never lowers, urgency when red-flag text exists.

## Valid departments
Emergency, Cardiology, Pulmonology, Neurology, General Medicine, Pediatrics, OBGYN, Orthopedics, Surgery, Gastroenterology, ENT, Ophthalmology, Dermatology, Psychiatry.
